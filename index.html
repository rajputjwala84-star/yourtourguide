Orchha Yatra Margdarshika (Orchha Full Guide)

Welcome to your guide for Orchha 🏰✨

"बेतवा के किनारे बैठकर, ढलती शाम देखना,
ओरछा आकर राम-राजा का दरबार देखना।
सुकून ढूँढने अगर निकले हो शहर की भीड़ से,
तो यहाँ के पत्थरों में छुपकर बैठा इतिहास देखना।" 🚩🌊🏰
1. Dharmik Sthal

Shri Ram Raja Temple

Duniya ka iklauta aisa mandir jahan Bhagwan Ram ki pooja Bhagwan ke roop mein nahi balki 'Raja' ke roop mein ki jaati hai. Yahan Madhya Pradesh Police dwara pratidin 'Guard of Honour' diya jaata hai.

⏱️ Darshan Ka Samay: Subah 8:00 - 12:30, Raat 8:00 - 10:30
2. Aitihasik Mahal

Orchha Fort & Jahangir Mahal

Raja Bir Singh Dev dwara Mughal Samrat Jahangir ke swagat mein banwaya gaya yeh mahal Bundela vaastukala ka bejod namoona hai. Ismein baareek jaaliyan aur shahi jharokhe hain.

🏛️ Ticket Fee: ₹10 - ₹250
3. Betwa Tat

Betwa Nadi Ki Chhatriyan (Royal Cenotaphs)

Kanchana Ghat ke kinare bani Bundela rajaon ki 14 bhavya chhatriyan. Shaam ko nadi ke paani mein inka pratibimb aur suryast ka nazara adbhut dikhta hai.

🌅 Uttam Samay: Suryast (Sunset)
4. Prachin Mandir

Chaturbhuj Temple

Vishaal meenaron wala yeh prachin mandir door se kile jaisa dikhta hai. Iski balcony se poore Orchha shahar ka khoobsurat nazara dikhai deta hai.
