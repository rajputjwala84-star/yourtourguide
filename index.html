Destination Guide

Welcome to your guide for Orchha 🏰✨

"Raja ram ki nagari hai, betwa ka shant kinara hai,
Orcha ke har ek patthar me, itihas ka sangeet pyara hai.
Chhatriyon ki parchhai jab pani me lahrati hai,
Ye shahar nahi saheb, ek khubsoorat afsana lagta hai." 🚩🌊
1. Dharmik Sthal

Shri Ram Raja Temple (श्री राम राजा मंदिर)

Duniya ka iklauta aisa mandir jahan bhagwan ram ko bhagwan ke roop me nahi balki 'raja' ke roop me pooja jata hai. Yahan madhya pradesh police dwara pratidin bhagwan ram ko 'guard of honour' (sashastra salami) diya jata hai.

⏱️ Darshan Time: 8:00 AM - 12:30 PM, 8:00 PM - 10:30 PM
2. Aitihasik Mahal

Orchha Fort & Jahangir Mahal (ओरछा किला एवं जहांगीर महल)

Raja bir singh dev dwara 17th century me mughal samrat jahangir ke swagat ke liye banaya gaya ye mahal bundela vastukala ka bejod namuna hai. Isme barik jaliyan, gumbad aur shahi jharokhe bane hue hain.

🏛️ Entry Fee Applicable
3. River View & Ghat

Royal Cenotaphs - Chhatris (बेतवा नदी की छतरियां)

Kanchana ghat ke kinare bani bundela rajaon ki 14 bhavya chhatriyan. Sham ke samay betwa nadi ke pani me inka pratibimb aur sunset ka nazara adbhut aur shant lagta hai.

🌅 Best Time: Sunset Hours
4. Prachin Mandir

Chaturbhuj Temple (चतुर्भुज मंदिर)

Vishal minaron aur oonche chabutaron wala ye prachin mandir door se kisi kile jaisa dikhta hai. Iski balcony se poore orchha shahar aur jahangir mahal ka panoramic view milta hai.

📍 Near Ram Raja Temple
5. Art & Wall Paintings

Laxmi Narayan Temple (लक्ष्मी नारायण मंदिर)

Ek chhoti pahadi par sthit ye mandir apni anuthi vastukala aur deewaron par bani 17th-18th century ki pauranik v aitihasik bhittichitron (wall paintings) ke liye prasiddh hai.

🎨 Amazing Historical Murals

💡 Travel Tips & Budget Guide (यात्रा सलाह)

Kab Jayein (Best Time): October se March ke beech mausam bahut suhavna rehta hai.
Rukna & Khana: Betwa tat ke paas saste homestay, budget hotel aur swadisht bundeli vyanjan asani se mil jaate hain.
